Privacy Policy — Chaowalit Greepoke Portfolio

Effective date: 2026-07-28 · Contact: bookchaowalit@gmail.com

Chaowalit Greepoke Portfolio ("the App") is a personal portfolio app that showcases projects, skills, and contact information. This policy explains what data the App does and does not handle.

Data we collect

None, on our servers. The App has no backend, no user accounts, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Specifically:

  • No personal data is transmitted to us or stored on any server we control.
  • Favorites and settings (theme, language, favorited projects) are stored only in local device storage and never leave your device.
  • Contact form opens your device's own email app with a pre-filled message. Whatever you choose to send is delivered through your own email provider — the App itself does not read, store, or transmit the message content.
  • Fonts: the App uses Google Fonts, which may fetch font files over the network from Google's font CDN on first use; this is a standard font-delivery request and is not linked to your identity by the App.
  • Links: tapping a project link opens it in your device's browser. Once you leave the App, the destination site's own privacy policy applies.
  • Deep links: the App supports opening specific screens via links, handled entirely on-device with no data collection.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No account creation or login
  • No location data
  • No contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash reporting
  • No third-party data sharing or selling

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected or stored outside your device, there is no account or data to delete on our end. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored preferences.

Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change (e.g. analytics or an account system are added in the future), this page will be updated with a new effective date before that change ships.
